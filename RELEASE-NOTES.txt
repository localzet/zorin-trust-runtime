Zorin Trust Runtime 5.0.5

Fixes pairing after the UI/service split. Runtime 5.0.4 correctly isolated TrustService in `:trust`, but the pairing screen still read and wrote process-local C globals. 5.0.5 adds an app-private cross-process state/command bridge, full-host-key-bound approval, and automatic TRUST-tab refresh. No phone identity or owner signer migration is required from 5.0.4.
